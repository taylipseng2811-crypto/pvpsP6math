Step 2 — Going Around

Circumference of a Circle

The circumference is the distance all the way around the circle. Think of it like the length of a fence around a round field.

THE FORMULA
C = π × d    or    C = 2 × π × r
where π ≈ 3.14 (or use 22/7 when the diameter/radius is a multiple of 7)

📝 Worked Example

A circle has a diameter of 14 cm. Find its circumference. (Use π = 22/7)
1
Identify what is given: Diameter = 14 cm
2
Write the formula: C = π × d
3
Substitute: C = 22/7 × 14
4
Simplify: 14 ÷ 7 = 2, so C = 22 × 2 = 44 cm
When diameter is a multiple of 7 (7, 14, 21, 28…), use π = 22/7. Otherwise use π = 3.14.

Step 3 — How Much Space?

Area of a Circle

The area is the space inside the circle. Think of painting the inside of a round plate — how much paint do you need?

THE FORMULA
A = π × r²
r² means r × r (radius times itself)
Area always uses the RADIUS (not diameter). If given a diameter, halve it first!

📝 Worked Example

A round pizza has a diameter of 28 cm. Find its area. (Use π = 22/7)
1
Given: Diameter = 28 cm, so Radius = 28 ÷ 2 = 14 cm
2
Formula: A = π × r²
3
A = 22/7 × 14 × 14
4
= 22/7 × 196 = 22 × 28 = 616 cm² ✅
Trick: 196 ÷ 7 = 28. So 22/7 × 196 = 22 × 28. Cancel the 7 first!

Step 4 — Parts of a Circle

Semicircle & Quarter Circle

Sometimes we only have half or a quarter of a circle. We just divide by 2 or 4!

🌗 Semicircle (Half Circle)

Curved part only:
= ½ × π × d
= π × r
Perimeter (full border):
= ½ × π × d + d
(curved part + straight edge)
Area of semicircle = ½ × π × r²
Perimeter of a semicircle = the curved part PLUS the straight diameter. Don't forget the straight edge!

📝 Worked Example

A semicircle has a diameter of 14 cm. Find its perimeter and area. (Use π = 22/7)
1
Diameter = 14 cm, Radius = 7 cm
2
Curved part = ½ × 22/7 × 14 = 22 cm
3
Perimeter = 22 + 14 = 36 cm ✅
4
Area = ½ × 22/7 × 7 × 7 = ½ × 154 = 77 cm² ✅

🍕 Quarter Circle

Curved part only:
= ¼ × π × d = ½ × π × r
Perimeter (full border):
= ¼ × π × d + 2r
(curved part + 2 straight edges)
Area of quarter circle = ¼ × π × r²

📝 Worked Example

A quarter circle has a radius of 7 cm. Find its perimeter and area. (Use π = 22/7)
1
Radius = 7 cm
2
Curved part = ¼ × 22/7 × 14 = ¼ × 44 = 11 cm
3
Perimeter = 11 + 7 + 7 = 25 cm ✅
4
Area = ¼ × 22/7 × 7 × 7 = ¼ × 154 = 38.5 cm² ✅

When to use 22/7 vs 3.14?

Use 22/7 when radius or diameter is a multiple of 7
e.g. r = 7, 14, 21, 28 cm
Use 3.14 for all other numbers
e.g. r = 5, 10, 6, 15 cm
The question will usually tell you which π to use. Always follow the instruction!

⚠️ Common Mistakes

!
Using diameter instead of radius in the Area formula. Area = π × r², NOT π × d²
!
Forgetting the straight edge in perimeter of semicircle/quarter circle
!
Forgetting to square the radius: r² = r × r (e.g. 7² = 49, NOT 14)
!
Mixing up cm (circumference) and cm² (area) in the answer units
